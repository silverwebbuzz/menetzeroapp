IFRS S2 climate disclosure

Al Maktoum Industries LLC · FY2025

Climate-related disclosures

Prepared in accordance with IFRS S2. Emissions figures are drawn from the FY2025 GHG inventory (GHG Protocol, operational control).

1. Governance

The Audit & Risk Committee holds delegated responsibility for climate-related risks and reviews performance quarterly. The Chief Sustainability Officer reports into the CEO and presents the inventory and target progress at each meeting.

2. Strategy

Carbon pricing and the phased tightening of DEWA tariff bands are the principal transition risks for the Jebel Ali site, assessed over a five-year horizon. Physical risk screening covers heat stress and coastal inundation at two coastal sites.

Section 2.3 (transition plan) is not yet answered — it will be omitted from the export until completed.
